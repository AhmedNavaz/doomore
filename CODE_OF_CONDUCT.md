# Code of Conduct

We want Doomore to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful, welcome different experience levels, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what helps the project.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so. Report problems privately through GitHub's "Report content" feature or by contacting the maintainer via the email on their GitHub profile.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
